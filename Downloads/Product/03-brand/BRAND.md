## Why this name
The object is the product. A manila folder is what a competent patient already builds from paper, slowly, after the damage. We made the folder first.

It is not a hospital-blue portal name. It is not an “AI advocate.” It is a piece of office stock.

**INFERENCE:** “Manila” will be confused with the city by some searchers. Acceptable. The wordmark is always “Manila File” on the site.

## Voice
- Short sentences. Paper words.
- Cite the statute or the page. Do not cite vibes.
- Never “empower,” “leverage,” “seamless,” “AI-powered.”
- Never invent a patient. If we tell Lisa Horan’s story, we name NBC Connecticut and the date.
- The enemy is the first number on the statement — not the nurse, not “doctors.”
- Humor is dry. No mascots.

**Yes:** Don't pay the first number.
