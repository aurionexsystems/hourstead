# Brand — Eavefile

## Name
**Eavefile** — the file you keep under the eave. The edge of the roof is what the satellite sees; the file is what you send back.

Checked on 2026-08-20 via RDAP `https://rdap.verisign.com/com/v1/domain/eavefile.com` → **HTTP 404 (unregistered / available)**. Not purchased (guardrail).

### Why not Groundsight
`groundsight.com` is registered (RDAP 200). The idea survived as the tagline.

### Rejected available names
`keptcover.com`, `lottrue.com`, `shinglecase.com`, `sixtyfile.com`, `noticepack.com`, `gablefile.com` — either benefit-generic or too cute. Eavefile is specific and a little strange. That is the point.

## Tagline
**Show them the ground.**

Alternates (do not use on the homepage; they are weaker): “Demand the photo.” / “The letter came from the sky.”

## Company
Eavefile. No LLC formed (would require a filing). Copyright line: `© 2026 Eavefile`.

## Voice
Calm, specific, slightly angry on the customer’s behalf, never cute. Short sentences. Cite or shut up. We do not say “empower.” We say “print the packet.”

We never promise a renewal. Bennett got one after a TV station called. We built the paperwork, not a miracle.

## Domain & handles
- Site: eavefile.com (available, not bought)
- App is a file, so the site can die and the product still works
- Social handles: **not reserved**. Do not post (guardrail). Check before launch: `@eavefile` on X is unverified.

## What we will not do
- Stock photos of smiling families on porches
- “AI-powered” in the headline (the *carrier* used the AI; we are the analog answer)
- Fear-mongering that aerial photos are illegal (NerdWallet/Passmore: usually legal)
