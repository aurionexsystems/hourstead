## Reddit r/homeowners (or r/Insurance)

**Title:** Got a “replace your roof or we won’t renew” letter and nobody came to the house — what did you actually send?

Body:

I keep seeing the aerial-photo non-renewals (State Farm / drone / satellite). ABC7 in March had Linda Bennett in Santa Ana: no inspector, $20k roof demand, “I thought they had the wrong house.” They renewed her after she pushed back. NerdWallet and United Policyholders both say the same three objects: current photos, a roofer’s report, the invoice if you already fixed it — and ask for the image + the date it was taken.

I got tired of assembling that in Word every time a neighbor forwarded a letter, so I made a single local HTML file that builds the packet and has sourced notice-day cards for CA/FL/TX/CT. No account. You can use the free tier.

I’m not a lawyer and I’m not saying this gets the policy back. Shop another carrier the same afternoon.

NAIC just put a number on the broader mess (Aug 5): 2,019,799 company-initiated HO nonrenewals in 2024.

**Title:** Show HN: Eavefile – a local HTML case file for aerial-imagery insurance non-renewals

Body:

The NAIC’s first countrywide MCAS cut (Aug 5, 2026) says insurers initiated 2,019,799 homeowners nonrenewals in 2024, with zone rates up 96–216% since 2018. A parallel, uglier slice of that is the drone/satellite condition letter: no inspector, a photo you were never shown, 30–120 days.

Eavefile is one HTML file. You type the notice, get a deadline clock, a state card that only includes rules I actually fetched (CA Ins. Code 678, Fla. Stat. 627.4133, TDI, Connecticut CID), an exhibit locker, and three drafts (demand the images, appeal, DOI complaint). Export is a JSON file you own. $49 one-time if you want the watermark gone; free tier is one case.

I did not stand up a server on purpose. The last company you want holding a scan of your roof is another SaaS.
