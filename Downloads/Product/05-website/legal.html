Legal and data

Postmark is a static letter file. It is not a law firm, a bank, a credit-repair service, or a subscription-cancel agency.

Flags citing ROSCA or California BPC § 17602 are screens. They do not decide that a statute applies to your merchant.

What we do not receive

There is no upload. There is no account. If you email us a .postmark file, a bank PDF, a card photo, or a medical record, that is a mistake — delete it; we will too, if an inbox ever exists. Support is product questions only.

The engine refuses Social Security–shaped and full-card–shaped strings. Last four digits are enough for an issuer note you send yourself.

This is not a HIPAA product. We do not do medicine. A prior medical-bill idea was killed for that reason.

License keys are an honor-system checksum. Buying a key (when checkout exists) is not a guarantee any merchant will stop.
