The extra is where the money walks off

California already wrote this down. A home-improvement extra is not enforceable against the buyer unless it is in writing, signed, and states scope, amount, and the effect on payments or completion — before that extra starts.

“An extra work or change order is not enforceable against a buyer unless the change order sets forth… the scope of work… the amount to be added or subtracted… the effect the order will make in the progress payments or the completion date.” Cal. Bus. & Prof. Code § 7159.6 — official legislative text, fetched 2026-08-20.
