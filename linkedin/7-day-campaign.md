# Hourstead — 7-Day LinkedIn Conversion Campaign

**Objective:** maximum license revenue ($79 Founding License) within 14 days. The 7 posts run daily (days 1–7); days 8–14 harvest via replies, DMs, and one re-share. Drafts only — nothing published, per guardrail.

## Conversion mechanics (read before posting)
- **Funnel:** post → free app (no signup, instant value) → 3-client cap / invoice footer → $79 license. The free product is the demo; every post drives an *open*, not a signup.
- **Founding framing = honest urgency.** "Founding License" is a real cohort: price locked at $79, all 1.x updates. Say that; never invent a deadline.
- **Link discipline:** LinkedIn suppresses posts with links. Put the link in the **first comment** within 60 seconds, and in your profile bio. Every post's CTA reads "link in first comment."
- **Cadence:** post 8:00–9:00 a.m. local (US audience), Tue–Mon. Reply to every comment within 2 hours for the first 6 hours — replies are the algorithm's fuel and the DMs are where licenses close.
- **DM follow-up (day 8–14):** anyone who comments "how does X work" gets a short DM: answer + "the free version has no expiry — if you outgrow 3 clients, the license is $79 once. Happy to answer anything." No pressure lines.
- **Numbers rule:** every stat in these posts is verified in `01-research/EVIDENCE.md`. Never post an unverified number; if week-one numbers are wanted for Day 7, use your real ones or none.

## Two-week profit math (target, not forecast)
LinkedIn-only, organic: 7 posts × ~1,500–3,000 impressions (realistic cold-start personal profile) → 2–4% profile/link clicks → 300–600 app opens → 10–15% hit the cap or want the footer gone → 30–60 warm prospects → 25–40% close = **8–24 licenses ≈ $630–$1,900 in 14 days.** Levers if tracking low by day 5: double down on the post archetype with the best comments; shift effort to DMs (highest close rate).

**Caveat this campaign cannot fix:** this is a funnel-conversion estimate, not a revenue guarantee — it assumes everyone who "closes" actually pays rather than bypassing the (unobfuscated, client-side-only) license check. See `07-proof/PROOF.md` → "The honest bear case" → License enforcement. Treat the range above as an upper bound on what good marketing can deliver, not a number the product mechanically enforces.
