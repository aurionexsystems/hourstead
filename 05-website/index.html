Fair questions

What happens if Hourstead shuts down or gets acquired?

Nothing happens to you. The app is a file on your computer and your data is a file on your computer. There is no server to turn off. That's not a promise of good behavior — it's an architecture that makes good behavior irrelevant.

Where exactly is my data?

In your browser's local storage on your machine, and in any stead files you export. It is never transmitted anywhere. There is no telemetry, no analytics, no account.

Can my small team use it?

Each person runs their own copy (one license each). Share work by exchanging stead files. There's no real-time sync — sync needs a server, and a server is a leash. If that's a dealbreaker, we're honestly not your tool.

Why should I trust a tool this cheap?

Because the economics are boring on purpose: one file, near-zero costs, no investors, no growth targets to hit. Companies rug-pull because they must. We can't — there's nothing to squeeze.

Is there a trial of the paid version?

The free version is the trial, and it never expires. Buy the license when you outgrow 3 clients or want the footer gone.
