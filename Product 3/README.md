# Jobfold — the finished package

**A local-first change-order and field-proof tool for small residential contractors.**

Jobfold makes an onsite extra visible before work starts: price it, describe it, document the request, get an acknowledgment, and keep a portable record. It deliberately avoids becoming another job-management subscription.

## Try it

Open [the app](04-product/jobfold.html) directly. It works without a server: create a change order, attach photos, draw an acknowledgment, print to PDF, and download a backup.

## Thesis

Small contractors have a repeatable payment-risk moment: a customer asks for “one more thing” onsite, but turning that into a priced, documented change order is enough friction that the work may begin without a record. Community advice repeatedly recommends a written change order before extra work; the MVP makes that specific action quick without asking a contractor to adopt a full CRM. All material claims and limits are documented in the evidence dossier.
