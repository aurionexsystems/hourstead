# Jobfold brand guide

## Positioning

**Jobfold** — “Make the extra work visible before you do it.”

The name evokes a folded job record: practical, field-ready, and less legalistic than “change order.” It is a working product name; no trademark clearance or domain availability claim has been made.

## Voice

Plain and non-accusatory. “Here’s what this adds” instead of “out of scope.” Never promise payment, dispute prevention, or legal enforceability.

## Visual system

- Ink `#17232C`: field notebook / structure
- Paper `#F7F3EB`: warm printable base
- Safety orange `#E6682E`: change / action
- Blueprint `#326F87`: secondary field cue
- Type: Georgia headlines; system sans for forms

Avoid glossy construction-tech blue, hard-hat stock photography, and legal-document visual language. The product should look usable on a dusty laptop and print cleanly in grayscale.
