# Askstead — Brand Guide

## Name
**Askstead** = ask + stead. Second house in the "stead" family (Hourstead was the first): tools for the ask, owned outright, never rented.
- Domain: **askstead.com — verified available 2026-07-10** via Verisign RDAP (404 = unregistered). Checked, not purchased. Also checked, taken: reviewstead.com, kudocard.com, praisekit.com, starstead.com.
- Studio note (option, inference): Hourstead + Askstead invite an umbrella indie studio ("Stead Works") selling own-it-outright tools to independents. Not claimed as a company; logged as the obvious next move.

## Tagline
**"The review is free. So is not renting a platform to ask for it."**
Short form: "Ask well. Own the tools."

## Story
Your best marketing is a happy customer holding their phone. The platforms charge $400 a month to intercept that moment — and the fine print keeps charging after you leave. Askstead is the moment, minus the platform: a printed card on your counter, a QR that opens your Google review form, a page that lets anyone tell you anything, publicly or privately. You buy it once. It's paper and a file. Nobody can repossess it.

## Voice
- Counter-talk, not dashboard-talk: "put this by the register," never "activate your engagement funnel."
- Compliance said plainly: "We don't do gating. Every customer sees both buttons. That's Google's rule and the FTC's — and it's also just honest."
- Price always visible. The category hides prices; we lead with ours.

## Visual identity
- **Logo:** speech-bubble plot with a five-point star seed — see `logo.svg` / `logo-wordmark.svg`.
- **Palette:** shares the stead-family soil/cream base, with its own primary:
  - Deep teal `#1F6E63` (primary — distinct from Hourstead's barn red)
  - Cream `#FAF6EF` (background)
  - Soil `#2B2118` (text)
  - Star gold `#D9A441` (stars, highlights — the shared family accent)
  - Clay `#C96F4A` (secondary accent)
- **Type:** Georgia/serif headlines, system sans UI (family style).
- Printables must look great in grayscale — most small businesses print on office laser printers. Test every asset in B/W.

## Naming conventions
- The output: a **kit**. The funnel page: the **counter page**.
- Releases named after storefront objects: 1.0 "Bell."
