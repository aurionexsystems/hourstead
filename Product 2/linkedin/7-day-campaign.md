## Day 2 (Wed) — The compliance wedge (reshare bait — the most important post of the week)

Review gating is banned, and in December the FTC started sending letters.

If you advise small businesses, this is worth two minutes:

— Google's policy: you CAN ask every customer for a review (a QR card by the register is explicitly fine). You CANNOT show the review link only to customers you think are happy, or pre-screen sentiment first. Google removed 292 MILLION policy-violating reviews last year.

— The FTC's reviews rule (in force since Oct 2024): no incentives for positive reviews, no suppression. First enforcement warning letters went out December 2025.

A lot of "reputation software" was built in the gating era. If your clients use a tool that filters unhappy customers away from Google — that's now a compliance question, not a growth hack.

The fix is honest and cheap: ask everyone, the same way, with a private-feedback door OFFERED ALONGSIDE the public one — never in front of it.
