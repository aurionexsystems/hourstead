## Post 1 — The compliance wedge (most reshareable; lead with this)

"Review gating" is banned, and the FTC started sending warning letters in December.

If you advise small businesses, worth two minutes:

→ Google's policy: you can ask every customer for a review (QR card on the counter — explicitly fine). You cannot pre-screen for happy customers before showing the Google link.
→ The FTC's reviews rule (in force since Oct 2024): no incentivized sentiment, no suppression. First enforcement letters went out Dec 2025.
→ Google says it blocked or removed 292 million policy-violating reviews last year.

A lot of "reputation management" tooling grew up doing exactly what's now banned.
