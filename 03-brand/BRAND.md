# Hourstead — Brand Guide

## Name
**Hourstead** = hour + stead (as in *homestead*: land you own and work yourself, not land you rent).
- Domain: **hourstead.com — verified available 2026-07-10** via Verisign RDAP (HTTP 404 = unregistered). Checked, not purchased, per guardrail. Also checked and taken: hourfile, ownhours, timegrain, hourkeep, keptbook, tallykeep, hourwright, keepsheet, hourloom (in redemption period — worth watching), ledgerloom, ledgerstead.
- Pronounceable, spellable, zero dictionary collisions in the category (Everhour is the nearest phonetic neighbor — distinct enough; noted as a trademark-search item for counsel before launch).

## Tagline
**"Your hours. Your file. Nobody's tenant."**
Alternates: "Stop renting your books." / "Time tracking you own outright."

## Story (the only pitch we ever give)
Every tool you rent can be sold out from under you. The people who bought your time tracker charge you per invoice now. Hourstead is the opposite of that: your entire business — hours, clients, invoices — lives in one file on your disk. Open it in any browser. Keep it forever. We sell you a license once, like a hammer, and then it's yours.

## Voice
- **Plainspoken.** Say "file," not "local-first data sovereignty." The homestead metaphor does the ideological work.
- **Calm, not angry.** We never name-and-shame competitors on the site; the reader already knows who burned them. "Some tools charge per invoice now" is enough.
- **Concrete promises only.** "Works offline. No account. Export everything, always." Never "revolutionary," "seamless," "supercharge."
- **First person singular OK.** Indie is the point. Sign things.

## Visual identity
- **Logo:** a barn-red square plot with a clock-hand furrow — see `logo.svg` (wordmark: `logo-wordmark.svg`).
- **Palette:**
  - Barn red `#A63A2B` (primary action, logo)
  - Cream `#FAF6EF` (background)
  - Soil `#2B2118` (text)
  - Wheat `#D9A441` (accents, highlights)
  - Sage `#7A8B6F` (success, paid status)
- **Type:** system serif for headlines (Georgia/`ui-serif` — deliberate anti-SaaS signal; every subscription tool uses geometric sans), system sans for UI (`system-ui`).
- **Texture:** generous whitespace, hairline rules, invoice-paper aesthetic. The brand should feel like good letterpress stationery, not a dashboard.

## Naming conventions
- The data file: a **stead** (`mybusiness.stead.json`).
- Paid tier: **Founding License**.
- Releases named after farm seasons: 1.0 "First Furrow."
